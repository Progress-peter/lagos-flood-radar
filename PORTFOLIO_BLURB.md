# Lagos Flood Radar — portfolio blurb

**Blurb (2–3 sentences)**

Lagos Flood Radar (2017–2025). I mapped satellite-detected flooding in Lagos State for nine rainy seasons using Sentinel-1 radar in Google Earth Engine, comparing every April–October image with that year's dry-season baseline. A land-cover check against ESA WorldCover removed about 40% of first-pass detections as lagoon surface or sand-filled ground. The results show 786–1,559 ha of flooded land each season, concentrated in Epe, Ibeju-Lekki and Badagry, and estimate the people, buildings and roads inside it. The findings are published as an interactive Leaflet map with a year-by-year view. Live map: https://lagos-flood-radar.vercel.app

**CV bullet**
